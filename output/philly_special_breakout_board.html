Method & checks

Scoring

Points = 2×1B + 3×2B + 4×3B + 5×HR + R + RBI + BB + HBP + 3×SB − E + 2×catcher CS + 2×OF assists (+5 grand slam, +10 cycle). Checked against Fantrax's own 2026 table: 500 of 500 hitters match except grand slams and cycles, which no public feed carries. No strikeout or caught-stealing penalty, so this league pays for singles, walks and steals more than most — which is why contact hitters like Arraez, Kwan and Yandy Díaz keep beating their price here.

Skills score — what the experts look at, in one number

  • Contact quality (weight 1.0): barrel%, hard-hit%, EV50, xwOBAcon — the Savant "red ink" Sarris and Scott White lean on.
  • Bat speed (0.75): average bat speed, fast-swing rate, ideal-attack-angle rate — Sarris's bat speed + swing shape, the "ideal fast swing" idea.
  • Approach (0.75): K%, BB%, chase%, whiff%, swing/take run value — a public stand-in for PitcherList's Decision Value / Contact components of Process+.
  • Speed (0.5): sprint speed. Steals are 3 points here.
  • Luck (0.5): xwOBA − wOBA. Positive means he hit the ball better than his line shows.
  • Trend (0.75): year-over-year change in barrel%, hard-hit%, K% and bat speed — the "Barrel Rate Test."
  • Age: 0.2 per year under 27.5, capped at ±1.2.

About 1.7× the base rate every year, and a positive average return where the field is flat. Real signal, not a crystal ball — roughly one in three of the top 40 will beat their price, versus one in six at random.
