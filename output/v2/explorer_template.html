2027 breakout candidates

BI is the Breakout Index: the model's probability that 2027 is new territory for the player (points per PA at least 0.08 above his best 200-PA season, 350+ PA, top-90 finish). It is trained on 2021→22 through 2025→26 and reads Statcast skills, expected-vs-actual points, career track, age, and IL history. An aging star cannot score high here by construction, which is the fix for the Trout problem. Proj is the separate points projection (skills model + empirical aging step, times a durability-aware PA estimate).

Keepers and the 2027 draft pool

You keep 5 hitters and the draft only holds what's left. KSV (Keeper Surplus Value) = projected 2027 points minus what the best hitter on the board at your first pick is projected to return (the (kept + 12)th best hitter). Positive means keeping him beats drafting; "lock" is 120+. Likely kept assumes each team keeps its five highest-KSV hitters. Real keeper lists are not published by Fantrax, so the past-year keepers are inferred as top-ADP hitters missing from the draft.

Prospects and rookies

PAS (Prospect Arrival Score, 0-100) = 35 × pedigree (MLB Pipeline rank) + 25 × age-vs-level + 25 × minor-league bat (ISO, OBP, BB−K, steals, z-scored within level) + 15 × MLB sample quality (xwOBA, EV50, bat speed) when he has one. Hitters only; current Pipeline top-100 plus every rookie-eligible bat with a 2026 MLB or upper-minors line.

Formulas and checks

League points (verified against Fantrax)

xLP — expected league points

What a hitter deserved in your scoring, from Statcast expected stats. Hits come from xBA, extra-base hits from xISO split by the hitter's own doubles/triples/homers mix (regressed to the league mix over 40 XBH), walks/HBP/steals are skills so they stay actual, runs and RBI come from a ridge model on xOBP/xISO/xBA/speed (league-average lineup context).

xH = xBA × AB; xXBH = xISO × AB / (r2B + 2·r3B + 3·rHR); x1B = xH − xXBH x2B = xXBH·r2B; x3B = xXBH·r3B; xHR = xXBH·rHR xR/PA, xRBI/PA = ridge(xOBP, xISO, xBA, SB/PA, xHR/PA, sprint) xLP = Σ weight(event) × x(event) luck = LP − xLP (+ = out-hit his contact quality)

Check: across 987 consecutive 300-PA seasons, xLP/PA predicted the next year's points/PA with r = 0.52 versus r = 0.47 for actual points/PA.

LPAR — league points above replacement

fill C, SS, 2B, 3B, 1B, OF slots for every team with the best available hitters, then the UT slots; replacement(pos) = points of the best hitter still unrostered at that position (+2 bench bats) LPAR = points − min(replacement over the positions he qualifies at, replacement(UT))

Value over ADP

expected(rank) = isotonic( rolling median of points by preseason ADP hitter rank ) per season pts_over_exp = points − expected(ADP rank); rank_gain = ADP rank − final rank beat: ≥ +75 pts, ≥ +25 ranks, top-150 finish big beat: ≥ +150, ≥ +60, top-75

Aging curve (empirical, delta method)

Change in points/PA from age a to a+1 for hitters with 300+ PA both years, PA-weighted, smoothed, cumulated relative to 27. Your league's scoring rewards singles and steals, both of which age badly, so the curve turns down early and hard.

Durability

projected PA = (0.6 × PA_2026 + 0.4 × mean PA 2024-26) × (1 − 0.0008 × IL days over last 3 years, capped 250) × (0.95 if age ≥ 33), clipped 200-650

Breakout Index

breakout(t+1) := pts/PA(t+1) ≥ career-best(200+ PA seasons through t) + 0.08 AND PA(t+1) ≥ 350 AND final rank(t+1) ≤ 90 BI = 100 × P(breakout | Statcast skills, xLP gap, career best, gap to best, seasons, age, PA, IL history, yoy K%/barrel/bat-speed change) gradient-boosted classifier, 2021→22 … 2025→26

Keeper Surplus Value

KSV = proj_pts − proj_pts[ rank = hitters kept league-wide + 12 ] (worth keeping over your first pick) KSV_2nd = proj_pts − proj_pts[ rank = kept + 24 ] (worth keeping over your second pick)

League fit

fit_vs_points = percentile(pts/PA, this scoring) − percentile(pts/PA, ESPN-style points with K = −1) fit_vs_roto = percentile(pts/PA, this scoring) − percentile(5x5 roto z-score)

Prospect Arrival Score

PAS = 35·pedigree + 25·level_age + 25·milb_bat + 15·mlb_sample pedigree = 1 − (Pipeline rank − 1)/100 (0.15 unranked); level_age = clip(0.5 + (avg age at level − age)·0.15, 0, 1) × level weight (AAA 1, AA .85, A+ .6, A .4) milb_bat = 0.5 + 0.2·z( ISO, OBP, BB−K, ½·SB/600 ) within level; mlb_sample = mean percentile of xwOBA, EV50, bat speed
