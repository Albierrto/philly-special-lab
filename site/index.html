2027 breakout candidates

BI is the Breakout Index: the model's probability that 2027 is new territory for the player (points per PA at least 0.08 above his best 200-PA season, 350+ PA, top-90 finish). It is trained on 2021→22 through 2025→26 and reads Statcast skills, expected-vs-actual points, career track, age, and IL history. An aging star cannot score high here by construction, which is the fix for the Trout problem. Proj is the separate points projection (skills model + empirical aging step, times a durability-aware PA estimate).

Starting pitchers

Every starter-season since 2021 (5+ starts) in your pitching scoring: IP + K − ER + 4×QS + 10×CG + 8×SHO − 3×BS, no RP slot, ten starts a week. The three "plus" columns are public-data proxies built the way PitcherList's Pitching+ and Eno Sarris's Stuff+/Location+ are framed: Stuff+ reads whiff, in-zone miss, put-away, velocity, movement uniqueness and arsenal run value; Location+ reads edge, first-strike, zone, meatball, walk and chase rates; Pitching+ is the ridge blend that best predicts next year's points per start. 100 is league average, 10 is one standard deviation. BI = chance next season is a new career level in points per start.

Keepers and the 2027 draft pool

You keep 5 hitters and 2 pitchers and the draft only holds what's left. KSV (Keeper Surplus Value) = projected 2027 points minus what the best player on the board at your first pick is projected to return (the (kept + 12)th best at that slot type). Positive means keeping him beats drafting; "lock" is 120+ for hitters and 80+ for pitchers. Likely kept assumes each team keeps its highest-KSV players. Your real 2026 keepers are marked; Ohtani is a pitcher keeper for you, so his hitting projection is added to his pitcher-slot value and he is left off your hitter board. Other teams' past keepers are inferred as top-ADP players missing from the draft.

Formulas and checks

League points (verified against Fantrax)

xLP — expected league points

What a hitter deserved in your scoring, from Statcast expected stats. Hits come from xBA, extra-base hits from xISO split by the hitter's own doubles/triples/homers mix (regressed to the league mix over 40 XBH), walks/HBP/steals are skills so they stay actual, runs and RBI come from a ridge model on xOBP/xISO/xBA/speed (league-average lineup context).

xH = xBA × AB; xXBH = xISO × AB / (r2B + 2·r3B + 3·rHR); x1B = xH − xXBH x2B = xXBH·r2B; x3B = xXBH·r3B; xHR = xXBH·rHR xR/PA, xRBI/PA = ridge(xOBP, xISO, xBA, SB/PA, xHR/PA, sprint) xLP = Σ weight(event) × x(event) luck = LP − xLP (+ = out-hit his contact quality)

Check: across 987 consecutive 300-PA seasons, xLP/PA predicted the next year's points/PA with r = 0.52 versus r = 0.47 for actual points/PA.

LPAR — league points above replacement

fill C, SS, 2B, 3B, 1B, OF slots for every team with the best available hitters, then the UT slots; replacement(pos) = points of the best hitter still unrostered at that position (+2 bench bats) LPAR = points − min(replacement over the positions he qualifies at, replacement(UT))

Value over ADP

expected(rank) = isotonic( rolling median of points by preseason ADP hitter rank ) per season pts_over_exp = points − expected(ADP rank); rank_gain = ADP rank − final rank beat: ≥ +75 pts, ≥ +25 ranks, top-150 finish big beat: ≥ +150, ≥ +60, top-75

Aging curve (empirical, delta method)

Change in points/PA from age a to a+1 for hitters with 300+ PA both years, PA-weighted, smoothed, cumulated relative to 27. Your league's scoring rewards singles and steals, both of which age badly, so the curve turns down early and hard.

Durability

projected PA = (0.6 × PA_2026 + 0.4 × mean PA 2024-26) × (1 − 0.0008 × IL days over last 3 years, capped 250) × (0.95 if age ≥ 33), clipped 200-650

Breakout Index

breakout(t+1) := pts/PA(t+1) ≥ career-best(200+ PA seasons through t) + 0.08 AND PA(t+1) ≥ 350 AND final rank(t+1) ≤ 90 BI = 100 × P(breakout | Statcast skills, xLP gap, career best, gap to best, seasons, age, PA, IL history, yoy K%/barrel/bat-speed change) gradient-boosted classifier, 2021→22 … 2025→26

Prospect Arrival Score

PAS = 35·pedigree + 25·level_age + 25·milb_bat + 15·mlb_sample pedigree = 1 − (Pipeline rank − 1)/100 (0.15 unranked); level_age = clip(0.5 + (avg age at level − age)·0.15, 0, 1) × level weight (AAA 1, AA .85, A+ .6, A .4) milb_bat = 0.5 + 0.2·z( ISO, OBP, BB−K, ½·SB/600 ) within level; mlb_sample = mean percentile of xwOBA, EV50, bat speed

Prospect card (TJStats-style)

Each minor-league line is colored by its percentile against every hitter with 100+ PA at the same level in the same season. The Statcast block comes from Savant's minor-league search (Triple-A parks carry Hawk-Eye; a few lower-level parks too): exit velocity, EV90, max EV, hard-hit and barrel rates, sweet-spot and ground-ball rates, an estimated wOBA built from xwOBAcon plus walks and strikeouts, whiff and chase rates, and bat-tracking when Savant publishes it for that park (it did not for 2025-2026 minor-league feeds, so bat speed is usually absent). Percentiles there are within the prospect cohort for the same season, so "90th" means top decile of tracked prospects, not of all Triple-A hitters.

Pitching: Stuff+, Location+, Pitching+ proxies

FanGraphs' Stuff+/Location+/Pitching+ (Eno Sarris and Max Bay) and PitcherList's PLV/Pitching+ are pitch-level models on velocity, movement, release and location. Those inputs are not public at the per-pitch level for free, so these are season-level proxies built from the same ideas with Savant's leaderboard and pitch-arsenal tables, scaled 100 ± 10 like the originals. They are not the real thing; the check below is what they are worth in this league's scoring.

Stuff+ = 100 + 10·z[ z(whiff%) + z(in-zone swing-miss%) + z(arsenal put-away%) + z(fastball velo) + ½·z(movement uniqueness) + z(arsenal RV/100 vs hitters) ] / 5.5 Location+ = 100 + 10·z[ z(edge%) + z(first-strike%) + ½·z(zone%) − z(meatball%) − z(BB%) + z(chase%) ] / 5.5 Pitching+ = 100 + 10·z[ ridge(Stuff+, Location+, xERA, K−BB%) → next-year points per start ] (coefficients below) arsenal: pitch types with 10+% usage; put-away = K / 2-strike pitches; movement uniqueness = distance from league-average movement of the same pitch type; z-scores among starters with 50+ IP in the same season

Pitcher Breakout Index and projection

breakout(t+1) := pts/GS(t+1) ≥ career-best(10+ GS seasons through t) + 1.5 AND GS(t+1) ≥ 20 AND SP rank(t+1) ≤ 40 BI = 100 × P(breakout | Stuff+, Location+, Pitching+, xERA, K−BB%, whiff, velo, yoy stuff/velo change, age, GS, career best, gap to best, IL history) proj pts/GS = ridge(Pitching+ inputs, last 2 seasons) + aging step(age); proj GS = (0.6·GS + 0.4·3-yr mean) × (1 − 0.001·IL days 3y, cap 250) × (0.93 if age ≥ 34), clipped 12-32 proj pts = proj pts/GS × proj GS; two-way: keeper value = pitching proj + hitting proj (Ohtani) pitcher KSV = proj pts − proj pts[ rank = pitcher keepers league-wide + 12 ]
